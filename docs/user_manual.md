# TaskWhite Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **TaskWhite**, your AI-powered task management software. This simplified guide is designed to get you up and running immediately, focusing purely on creating, managing, and completing your tasks efficiently.

---

## 1. Getting Started

TaskWhite ensures a secure and seamless onboarding experience.

### Signup Process
Choose your preferred signup method:
- **Email Magic Link**: Enter your email address to receive a secure, one-time login link. No password required.
- **Google/Microsoft OAuth**: Click the respective button on the login screen to instantly authenticate via your existing provider.

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview

Your Dashboard is your central command center. It offers two distinct ways to view your tasks:

- **List View**: A detailed table view showing all tasks, assignees, tags, due dates, and statuses. Great for bulk actions.
- **Calendar View**: A visual monthly layout showing exactly when tasks are due. *Tip: You can drag and drop tasks across dates in the calendar view to instantly reschedule them!*

> **Toggle Views**: You can quickly switch between List and Calendar views using the toggle buttons at the top of the tasks list, or by pressing `v` on your keyboard.

---

## 3. Creating & Categorizing Tasks

### How to Create a Task
1. Click the **+ New Task** button (or press `c` or `n` on your keyboard).
2. **Client**: Select the client this task belongs to (or choose "Internal").
3. **Task Type**: Choose from your standard task types (e.g., "Video Editing", "Content Writing").
4. **Assign To**: Select a team member to take ownership.
5. **Due Date**: Pick the deadline.
6. **Tags**: (See below).
7. Click **Create Task(s)**.

### Adding Tags
Tags allow you to add custom, colorful categorizations to your tasks (e.g., "Urgent", "Podcast", "Mock-data").
- In the "Tags" field, simply type your tag name and press **comma (`,`)** or **Enter**.
- The tag will instantly appear as a badge. You can add as many tags as you need.

---

## 4. Managing & Completing Tasks

### Changing Task Status
Every task flows through a simple lifecycle: `Assigned` → `In Progress` → `Completed` (or `Overdue`).
- **Inline Editing**: In the List View, you can click on the `Status` column of any task to quickly change its status.
- **Full Edit**: Click the Edit icon (or press `e` while focused on a task) to open the task details and change the status from the dropdown.

### Bulk Actions
If you need to mark multiple tasks as completed or delete them simultaneously:
1. Check the boxes next to the tasks in the List View (or press `Space` to check a focused row).
2. A bulk action bar will appear at the top.
3. Select **Complete** or **Delete**.

*(Completed tasks are automatically moved to your **Task Archives** after a short delay based on your Auto-Archive rules.)*

---

## 5. Keyboard Shortcuts (Power User Guide)

TaskWhite is built for speed. Use these global keyboard shortcuts to navigate the app without touching your mouse:

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `c` or `n` | Open the **Create New Task** modal |
| `/` | Focus the **Search** bar to find tasks quickly |
| `v` | Toggle between **List** and **Calendar** views |
| `Arrow Up/Down` | Navigate through tasks in the List View |
| `Space` | Select/Deselect the checkbox of the currently focused task |
| `e` or `Enter` | Edit the currently focused task |
| `m` | Quickly mark the selected (or focused) task(s) as **Completed** |
| `Delete` or `Backspace` | Delete the selected (or focused) task(s) |

> **Pro Tip**: Use the Arrow keys to move down your list, hit `Space` on a few tasks, and press `m` to complete them all instantly!
