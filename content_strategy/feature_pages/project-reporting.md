# Project Reporting

## Overview
Make data-driven decisions with TaskWhite's powerful Project Reporting tools. Visualize progress, track time, and identify bottlenecks before they impact your deadlines.

## Benefits
*   **Data-Driven Decisions:** Move away from guesswork and rely on real-time metrics.
*   **Identify Roadblocks:** Spot delayed tasks and uneven workloads instantly.
*   **Client Transparency:** Generate professional reports to share progress with stakeholders.

## Examples
*   **Sprint Retrospectives:** Analyze completed work vs. planned work to improve future sprints.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Balance team workloads by viewing capacity across multiple projects.
*   **Executive Dashboards:** Provide high-level summaries of portfolio health to leadership.

## Steps to Get Started
1.  **Navigate to Reports:** Click the "Reports" tab in the main navigation menu.
2.  **Choose a Template:** Select a pre-built report or start from scratch.
3.  **Customize Widgets:** Add charts, graphs, and tables to visualize your data.
4.  **Export and Share:** Download as PDF/CSV or schedule automated email deliveries.

## Best Practices
*   **Define KPIs:** Know what metrics matter most to your team before building reports.
*   **Keep it Simple:** Avoid cluttering dashboards with unnecessary information.
*   **Review Regularly:** Schedule weekly or monthly reviews to track trends over time.

## FAQs
**Q: Can I customize the colors and branding on reports?**
A: Yes, Enterprise plans allow for custom branding on exported reports.

**Q: Can I track time spent on specific tasks?**
A: Yes, time tracking data automatically feeds into your reporting dashboards.
