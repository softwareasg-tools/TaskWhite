## Feature Deep Dive

### Ease of Use & Learning Curve
**Jira** is notoriously difficult to master. The creation of custom workflows, issue types, screens, and permission schemes offers unparalleled power but immense complexity. Non-technical users often find it overwhelming.
**TaskWhite** prioritizes a frictionless user experience. It offers powerful workflow customization through an intuitive visual builder. Marketing and sales teams feel just as comfortable in TaskWhite as engineers do, ending the silo between departments.

### Reporting and Analytics
**Jira** provides deep agile reporting: velocity charts, burndown/burnup charts, epic reports, and control charts. It is the gold standard for Scrum Masters.
