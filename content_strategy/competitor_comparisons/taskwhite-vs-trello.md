# TaskWhite vs Trello: Which Project Management Tool Should You Choose?

## Executive Summary
**In short:** Choose **Trello** if you need a basic, lightweight Project Board for a small team or personal projects and do not require advanced reporting or complex task dependencies. Choose **TaskWhite** if you need a scalable project management platform that combines intuitive task tracking with advanced reporting, and cross-project visibility without sacrificing ease of use.

## Who is it For?
*   **TaskWhite:** Growing teams, mid-market businesses, and agencies that need structure, multiple views (Gantt, List, Calendar), and detailed reporting out-of-the-box.
*   **Trello:** Individuals, freelancers, and small teams that only need a simple visual drag-and-drop board for linear workflows.

## Feature Overview
While Trello relies heavily on its "Power-Ups" ecosystem to add functionality to its core List Views offering, TaskWhite provides a native, all-in-one suite of project management tools. TaskWhite supports multiple views including List, Timeline, and Gantt by default.

## Ease of Use & Learning Curve
*   **Trello:** Incredibly low barrier to entry. The UI is instantly understandable.
*   **TaskWhite:** Designed with an intuitive interface that balances power with simplicity. It requires slightly more setup than Trello but offers onboarding flows that get teams up and running in minutes, eliminating the steep learning curves typical of enterprise software.

## Reporting & Analytics
*   **TaskWhite:** Offers real-time dashboards, resource utilization metrics, and time-tracking reports natively. You can easily visualize cross-project progress.
*   **Trello:** Native reporting is virtually non-existent on basic tiers. You must rely on third-party Power-Ups or export data to external tools to get meaningful insights.

## Collaboration & Communication
Both tools offer robust tracking systems and @mentions. TaskWhite edges ahead by offering centralized proofing and record collaboration alongside tasks, reducing context switching.

## Mobile Experience
Both platforms offer highly rated iOS and Android apps. Trello's app is excellent for quickly moving cards, while TaskWhite's app allows for deeper task management, reporting views, and team communication on the go.

## Pros and Cons

### TaskWhite
**Pros:**
*   Highly scalable for growing teams.
*   Rich native feature set (no need for dozens of plugins).
*   Advanced real-time reporting.

**Cons:**
*   May be overkill for a simple personal to-do list.

### Trello
**Pros:**
*   Familiar, highly visual interface.
*   Excellent free tier for basic needs.
**Cons:**
*   Clunky when handling complex, multi-step projects.
*   Can get expensive when you need multiple Power-Ups.
*   Lacks native advanced reporting.

## Simple, Flat Pricing

Unlike other platforms that gate essential tools behind expensive enterprise plans, TaskWhite offers a simple, transparent flat-pricing model. Every feature—including advanced reporting, custom tags, and all project views—is available at all levels. You get the full power of the platform without the upsell.

## Switching Considerations
Migrating from Trello to TaskWhite is straightforward using CSV imports or native migration tools. Because Trello's data structure is flat (Board > List > Card), mapping it to TaskWhite's structured hierarchy will instantly give you better visibility into your projects.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Is TaskWhite better than Trello?**
TaskWhite is generally better for teams that need comprehensive project management, multiple views, and native reporting. Trello is better for users looking for a strictly visual, lightweight Project Board.

**Can I import my Trello boards to TaskWhite?**
Yes, TaskWhite supports seamless data imports from Trello, allowing you to bring over your boards, lists, and cards without losing data.

**Does Trello have Gantt charts?**
Trello does not have native Gantt charts on its free or standard plans. You must upgrade to Premium for Timeline views or use third-party Power-Ups. TaskWhite includes these views natively.

## Final Recommendation
If your team's workflow is simple and strictly linear, **Trello** is a fantastic, lightweight choice. However, if you are looking to scale, need actionable insights from your data, require multiple ways to view projects, and want modern workflows, **TaskWhite** provides a much more powerful and future-proof solution.
