# TaskWhite for Sales Teams

Closing deals requires persistence, organization, and perfect timing. TaskWhite helps sales teams move beyond simple CRMs by providing actionable task management to track follow-ups, onboard clients, and collaborate with other departments.

## Common Workflow Challenges in Sales

*   **Dropped Follow-ups:** Leads slip through the cracks when follow-up tasks aren't tracked systematically.
*   **Messy Handoffs:** Transitioning a closed won deal to the customer success or implementation team often lacks critical context.
*   **Administrative Overload:** Spending too much time updating spreadsheets instead of selling.
*   **Lack of Visibility:** Sales managers struggle to see what tasks their reps are working on daily beyond just pipeline value.

## How TaskWhite Can Help

*   **Lead Tracking Boards:** Visualize leads moving through different stages using Project Boards.
*   **Task Reminders:** Set due dates and recurring tasks to ensure timely follow-ups with prospects.
*   **Streamlined Handoffs:** Create standardized checklists for onboarding new clients, ensuring all necessary information is collected and shared with the relevant teams.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Easily tag legal or product teams on tasks to get quick answers to prospect questions.

## Example Workflows

1.  **Lead Qualification:** Track incoming leads, assign them to reps, and guide them through the qualification steps.
2.  **Contract Negotiation:** Manage the back-and-forth of legal reviews and approvals with internal teams.
3.  **Customer Onboarding:** A step-by-step checklist to ensure a smooth transition from sales to customer success.

## FAQs

**Q: Is TaskWhite a replacement for my CRM?**
A: TaskWhite is designed to complement your CRM. While a CRM holds customer data, TaskWhite manages the specific daily actions, collaborative tasks, and internal processes required to close deals.

**Q: Can I automate task creation for new leads?**
A: Yes, you can use basic rules to create standardized tasks when a lead moves to a specific stage in your process.
