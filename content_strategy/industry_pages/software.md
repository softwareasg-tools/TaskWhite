# TaskWhite for Software Development & IT

Building great software requires alignment between product managers, designers, and engineers. TaskWhite provides the flexibility to run agile sprints, manage product roadmaps, and track bugs without the steep learning curve of complex developer tools.

## Common Workflow Challenges in Software Teams

*   **Misaligned Priorities:** Engineers work on low-priority bugs while critical features are delayed.
*   **Poor Cross-Team Visibility:** Marketing and Sales have no idea when new features will actually be released.
*   **Cluttered Backlogs:** Feature requests and bug reports pile up without a clear system for prioritization or triage.
*   **Inefficient Sprint Planning:** Planning meetings take too long because tasks lack clear descriptions and point estimates.

## How TaskWhite Can Help

*   **Agile Boards:** Use Kanban boards to manage sprints, moving tasks from "To Do" to "In Progress" to "Code Review" and "Done."
*   **Product Roadmaps:** Visualize long-term epics and initiatives on a timeline to share with stakeholders.
*   **Bug Tracking:** Create a dedicated project to log, triage, and assign bugs with custom fields for severity and environment.
*   **Clear Specifications:** Use task descriptions and attachments to provide engineers with clear requirements, designs, and acceptance criteria.

## Example Workflows

1.  **Sprint Management:** Plan and execute two-week sprints, assigning tasks to developers and tracking progress daily.
2.  **Bug Triage:** A systematic process for QA and customer support to log issues, which product managers can then prioritize for the engineering team.
3.  **Feature Launch Coordination:** Manage the technical deployment alongside marketing activities and sales enablement.

## FAQs

**Q: Can TaskWhite support Agile methodologies?**
A: Yes, TaskWhite's flexible boards and custom fields make it easy to implement Scrum or Kanban workflows, track story points, and manage backlogs.

**Q: How does TaskWhite help bridge the gap between engineering and non-technical teams?**
A: By keeping project management in an accessible, intuitive platform, teams like Marketing and Customer Success can easily view the product roadmap and monitor the status of bug fixes without needing to learn complex developer tools.
