## Common Workflow Challenges in the Legal Industry

Legal professionals navigate highly demanding environments:
*   **Deadline Management:** Tracking court dates, filing deadlines, and statute of limitations critical to case success.
*   **Document Organization:** Managing vast amounts of case files, evidence, briefs, and contracts securely.
*   **Team Coordination:** Ensuring seamless collaboration between partners, associates, paralegals, and administrative staff.
*   **Client Intake and Updates:** Streamlining the onboarding of new clients and keeping them informed of case progress.
*   **Time and Billing Accuracy:** Precisely tracking billable hours associated with specific case tasks.
