# TaskWhite for Human Resources

Human Resources is the backbone of any organization, handling sensitive information and critical processes. TaskWhite provides a secure, organized environment to manage recruitment, onboarding, and employee development.

## Common Workflow Challenges in HR

*   **Inconsistent Onboarding:** New hires receive different experiences depending on their manager, leading to confusion and slow ramp-up times.
*   **Lost Applicant Data:** Resumes and interview notes get scattered across emails and local drives.
*   **Compliance Tracking:** Ensuring all necessary forms and training modules are completed by deadlines is difficult without automated tracking.
*   **Managing Cross-Department Tasks:** Onboarding requires coordination with IT for equipment and Finance for payroll, which often leads to bottlenecks.

## How TaskWhite Can Help

*   **Standardized Checklists:** Create repeatable templates for onboarding and offboarding to ensure nothing is missed.
*   **Applicant Tracking Pipeline:** Use a visual board to move candidates through resume review, phone screens, and interviews.
*   **Confidential Workspaces:** Set permissions and privacy controls to ensure sensitive HR data is only visible to authorized personnel.
*   **Cross-Department Coordination:** Assign tasks to IT and Finance directly within the onboarding project so everything is ready for the new hire's first day.

## Example Workflows

1.  **Applicant Tracking:** Manage job postings, review resumes, and coordinate interview schedules.
2.  **Employee Onboarding:** A comprehensive 30-60-90 day plan for new hires, including equipment setup, training, and introductions.
3.  **Performance Review Cycles:** Track the status of self-evaluations and manager reviews across the company.
