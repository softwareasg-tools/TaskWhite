## Common Workflow Challenges in Ecommerce

Ecommerce teams must move quickly and accurately to stay competitive:
*   **Product Launch Coordination:** Aligning product data, photography, web development, and marketing copy for a simultaneous go-live.
*   **Marketing Campaign Execution:** Managing fast-paced social media schedules, email blasts, and ad campaigns.
*   **Cross-Functional Silos:** Disconnects between the inventory/fulfillment teams and the marketing/sales teams.
*   **Website Updates & Bug Tracking:** Managing continuous improvements, feature requests, and technical issues on the storefront.
*   **Vendor and Freelancer Management:** Coordinating work with external photographers, copywriters, or SEO agencies.
