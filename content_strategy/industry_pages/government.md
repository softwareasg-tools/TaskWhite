## Common Workflow Challenges in Government

Public sector workflows are often complicated by specific constraints:
*   **Siloed Departments:** Difficulty coordinating efforts and sharing information across different agencies or municipal departments.
*   **Complex Approvals:** Navigating multi-tiered review and approval processes that can delay project execution.
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensuring all projects and communications adhere to strict legal and policy guidelines.
*   **Transparency and Reporting:** Maintaining clear records of decisions and providing transparent reporting to stakeholders and the public.
*   **Legacy Systems:** Struggling with outdated, fragmented software that hinders productivity.
