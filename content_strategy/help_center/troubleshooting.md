# Troubleshooting Guide

Find solutions to common issues you might encounter while using TaskWhite.

## Loading Issues
If TaskWhite is slow or not loading properly:
1.  **Clear your browser cache and cookies.**
2.  **Try an incognito/private browsing window.**
3.  **Check our Status Page** to ensure there are no ongoing outages.

## Email Notification Problems
Not receiving emails?
1.  Verify your email address in Account Settings.
2.  Check your spam/junk folders.
3.  Ensure your IT department hasn't blocked emails from `no-reply@taskwhite.com`.

## Access Denied Errors
If you see an "Access Denied" message when clicking a link:
*   You may not have permission to view that specific project or task.
*   Contact the project owner or your Workspace Admin to request access.

## Desktop App Issues
If the desktop app is crashing:
1.  Ensure you are running the latest version.
2.  Uninstall and reinstall the application.

If you're still experiencing issues, please contact our support team.
