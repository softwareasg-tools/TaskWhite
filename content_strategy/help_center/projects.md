# Managing Projects

Projects are the core organizational unit in TaskWhite. Learn how to create, manage, and view your projects effectively.

## Project Views
TaskWhite offers multiple ways to visualize your work:
*   **List View:** A structured spreadsheet-like format.
*   **Board View (Kanban):** Great for tracking status across stages.
*   **Timeline View (Gantt):** Ideal for scheduling and dependencies.
*   **Calendar View:** See deadlines at a glance.

## Project Status Updates
Keep stakeholders informed by publishing regular status updates.
1. Go to the Project Overview tab.
2. Click **New Status Update**.
3. Choose a status (On Track, At Risk, Off Track) and provide a brief summary.

## Archiving Projects
When a project is complete, you can archive it to remove it from your active list while retaining the data.
*   Click the project menu (three dots) and select **Archive**.
