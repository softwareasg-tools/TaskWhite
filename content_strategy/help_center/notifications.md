# Managing Notifications

Stay informed without getting overwhelmed. Learn how to control your TaskWhite notifications.

## Types of Notifications
*   **Inbox:** In-app notifications for direct updates (mentions, assignments).
*   **Email:** Summaries and immediate alerts delivered to your inbox.
*   **Push:** Mobile and desktop alerts.

## Customizing Triggers
You can choose exactly what events trigger a notification.
Go to **Settings > Notifications** to toggle alerts for:
*   Task assignments
*   @mentions
*   Due date reminders
*   Status updates

## Do Not Disturb
Pause notifications when you need to focus.
*   Click your prorecord picture and select **Pause Notifications**.
*   Choose a duration (e.g., 1 hour, until tomorrow).

## Troubleshooting
Not receiving emails?
*   Check your spam folder.
*   Ensure your email address is verified in Account Settings.
