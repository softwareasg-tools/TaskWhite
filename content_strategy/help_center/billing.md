# Billing and Subscriptions

Manage your TaskWhite plan, payment methods, and invoices.

## Available Plans
TaskWhite does not gate features behind expensive tiers. All premium features are included on every plan.

*   **Free:** ₹0/mo (Up to 3 users)
*   **Starter:** ₹416/mo (Up to 5 users)
*   **Growth:** ₹1,249/mo (Up to 15 users)

### All Plans Include the Following Features:
*   **Unlimited Tasks:** Create as many tasks and projects as you need.
*   **Multiple Views:** Visualize work with Project Boards, List Views, and Timelines.
*   **Custom Tags:** Build dynamic virtual teams and organize cross-functional work.
*   **Task Dependencies:** Map out complex workflows with clear task blockers.
*   **Flexible Permissions:** Utilize Org View for transparency or My View for private tasks.
*   **Real-time Collaboration:** Discuss work where it happens with task-level updates.

## Upgrading Your Plan
1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**.
3. Choose your desired tier and billing cycle (Monthly or Annual).

## Managing Payment Methods
Update your credit card or billing details.
*   In the Billing section, click **Update Payment Method**.
*   We accept all major credit cards.

## Viewing Invoices
Download past receipts for your records.
*   Scroll down to the **Invoice History** section on the Billing page to download PDF invoices.
