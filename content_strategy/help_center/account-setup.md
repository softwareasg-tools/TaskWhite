# Account Setup

Customizing your TaskWhite account helps you work the way you want. Here’s how to configure your profile and preferences.

## Profile Settings
Update your personal information to help your team recognize you.
1. Click your avatar in the top right corner.
2. Select **My Profile**.
3. Upload a photo, add your job title, and update your bio.

## Notification Preferences
Control how and when you receive updates.
1. Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
2. Choose your preferred channels: Email, Desktop, or Mobile.
3. Customize triggers (e.g., only notify me when @mentioned).

## Security Settings
Keep your account secure.
*   Change your password regularly.
*   Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for added protection.
