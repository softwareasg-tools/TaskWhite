# Roles and Permissions

Control who can see and do what within your TaskWhite workspace.

## Workspace Roles
*   **Workspace Owner:** Full control over billing, settings, and users.
*   **Admin:** Can manage users and global settings, but cannot access billing.
*   **Member:** Standard user who can create projects and tasks.

## Project Permissions
Within a project, you can set access levels:
*   **Org View:** Projects visible to the entire organization, promoting transparency.
*   **My View:** Private projects visible only to you, perfect for personal task management.
*   **Org View + My View:** A hybrid approach allowing specific teams to collaborate privately while keeping other parts of the workspace open.

## Changing Permissions
Workspace Admins can adjust user roles from the **Members** tab in Workspace Settings.
