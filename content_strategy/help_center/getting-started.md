# Getting Started with TaskWhite

Welcome to TaskWhite! This guide will walk you through the basics of setting up your workspace and starting your first project.

## 1. Navigating the Interface
The TaskWhite interface is designed for simplicity.
*   **Sidebar:** Access your Home, My Tasks, Projects, and Teams.
*   **Top Bar:** Global search, notifications, and profile settings.
*   **Main View:** Where your project data lives (List, Board, Timeline, etc.).

## 2. Creating Your First Project
A project is where you organize related tasks.
1. Click the **+ New Project** button.
2. Choose a template or start from scratch.
3. Name your project and set the privacy level.

## 3. Adding Tasks
Tasks represent individual pieces of work.
*   Click **Add Task** in your new project.
*   Give it a clear, actionable title.
*   Assign it to yourself or a team member.
